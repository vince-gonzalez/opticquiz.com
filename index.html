Sharpness, speed & perception
Contrast SensitivityNew
Eye charts test sharpness; this tests the faint — a letter fading toward the background until it nearly vanishes. A different axis of vision, and often the first to slip.
Fading letters · Adaptive staircase · Log contrast · This-screen baseline

Vernier AcuityNew
Two short lines; the lower is nudged a hair left or right. Call the direction. Humans detect misalignments finer than a single cone — this finds your hyperacuity limit.
2AFC staircase · Sub-pixel offsets · Arc-second estimate
→
Reaction TimeNew
Red field, then — the instant it flashes green — tap. We measure the gap five times and report your best and average in milliseconds. Tap early and the trial resets.
5 trials · Millisecond timing · Best + average
→
Flicker FusionNew
A dot flickers black-to-bright. Slow, you see it blink; fast enough, it fuses to a steady glow. The speed where blinking vanishes is your critical flicker fusion threshold.
Slider staircase · Refresh-aware cap · Threshold in Hz

Important — Read Before Using
OpticQuiz is an educational wellness screening tool, not a medical device, diagnostic instrument, or licensed healthcare service. Results are probabilistic estimates based on population-level statistical models intended for personal informational and educational purposes only.

OpticQuiz does not diagnose any medical condition. OpticQuiz does not provide medical advice, clinical guidance, or treatment recommendations. OpticQuiz is not a substitute for examination by a licensed optometrist or ophthalmologist. Screen-based tests are fundamentally limited by display calibration, color profile, ambient lighting, and viewing distance.

If you are concerned about your vision or eye health, consult a licensed eye care professional. Use of this tool constitutes acceptance that results are for personal educational reference only and carry no clinical weight.
