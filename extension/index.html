HOW IT WORKS

Click the toolbar eye and choose a mode. The page's colors are re-mapped so the differences your vision normally compresses become visible again (daltonization, via an in-browser color filter). Your choice is remembered as you browse.

It corrects, it doesn't simulate — the point is to help you see, not to show sighted people what color blindness looks like. It runs on the same published method as the rest of OpticQuiz: Machado (2009) simulation, scored with CIEDE2000.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No tracking. No network requests. No account. Everything happens in your browser; your chosen mode is stored only on your device. See the full privacy policy.

Honest scope. Correction is a powerful aid, not a cure — it strongly improves color separation but won't resolve every case. Because it applies one filter to the page, sites that rely on position:fixed/sticky elements may shift while a mode is on; switch to Off to restore. Method: published, open-access.
